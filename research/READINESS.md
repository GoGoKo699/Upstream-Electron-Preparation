## 2. What is supported, and what is not

The formal precedent benchmark is met at the declared reading depths. It is
not five unrelated derivations of every equation: some sources construct the
model, some use it, and some print an equivalent Hamiltonian or amplitude that
is translated explicitly. Publications recur across premises and share
theoretical ancestry. These counts must not be added into an independent
confirmation score.

The experimental claim is separate. There are zero complete joint-regime
demonstrations in the inspected pool, and no uniform apparatus regime as
fidelity tends to one has been established. Finite temperature, dispersion,
attenuation, additional modes, control bandwidth and measurement limitations
remain real qualifications. The formal count does not waive an experimental
standard or establish a laboratory operating window. A conditional theorem
does not require such a demonstration; a device-performance claim would.

The contribution is the complete, attainable energy–full-state-fidelity law
for the fixed one-input task. General pulse shaping, interaction compensation,
coherent propagation and two-input protection are inherited. Quadratic
completion and the simple-zero singular exponent are standard mathematics.
The manuscript must explain why the restricted-control state-preparation law
is useful without claiming a new optimization method or a universal cost of
electron creation. The [significance assessment](SIGNIFICANCE.md) supports
that focus but does not establish broad impact.

## 3. Fixed dependency chain and stopping point

Keep two open channels, zero temperature, elastic linear lossless
dispersionless bosonic scattering, one deterministic real charge-one
$L^1\cap L^2$ input of either sign, a fixed Lorentzian electron plus sea, and
an unrestricted unused output. The budget is excess energy launched into the
incoming driven edge and therefore includes both eventual outputs. It is not
full circuit work or irreversible heat. Whole-line tails are allowed; finite
relative norm is proved for the optimizers, not assumed for all competitors.

C1–C2 carry the argument. Optimal-output energy allocation and the matched-error
control comparison interpret it. C4's more general exact-target classification
and C5's nominal duration/calibration results remain subordinate. C3's
separately unaudited hole inequality remains outside the manuscript dependency
chain. No new detector, thermal, dissipative, finite-start or robust-control
theorem is needed to finish this story.

Stop the pre-manuscript research after integration, review and source-matched
verification. Reopen it only for a concrete counterexample, a potentially
covering result, or a changed claim whose assumptions need new evidence.
External critical reading remains desirable and absent; another assistant
pass must not be described as providing it. The next bounded scientific
action is drafting the scoped argument if requested, not another general
survey or an automatic expansion of the model.
