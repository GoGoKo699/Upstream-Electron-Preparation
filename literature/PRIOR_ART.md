## Resolved and unresolved distinctions

The active statement “Cabart: abstract only” is obsolete. It survives only in the immutable historical scout records. The supplied-paper audit does not prove global priority or close experimental implementation. Likewise, R21 contains optimization, and L11 contains exact many-body restoration: neither should be dismissed by a broad claim that prior work only studied decoherence.

The strongest remaining objection is that the complete result may be viewed as regularized inversion of an inherited filter after the correct quantum objective is identified. That is a significance/priority question, not something resolved by more digits or test counts. A covering theorem in an uninspected source remains possible. Further source checks should name the claim they could subsume.

This import performs no new broad literature search and does not certify a single tutorial as an adopted prerequisite. The source records in [the archive](../archive/README.md) state all earlier retrieval failures and actual depths; failed requests were not negative novelty evidence. Readiness and independent scientific review remain different assessments.
