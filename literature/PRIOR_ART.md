The state, transfer model, coherent-state mathematics and quadratic optimization tools are inherited. Each comparison is tied to the passages and reading depth stated below.

## How the comparisons fit together

L11 treats many-body restoration, R21 optimizes source quality, and C18 supplies
the open-channel dynamics and a changed-geometry protection mechanism. The
[control comparison](CONTROL_PRIOR_ART.md) also attributes prescribed-current
synthesis to M18, calibrated precompensation to B19 and two-input protection to
R20. The contribution studied here is the exact preparation law for the fixed
control, target, energy and fidelity specified in the [claim map](../research/MODEL_AND_CLAIMS.md).
Its quadratic regularized-inversion method is standard.

The [precedent map](PRECEDENT_MAP.md) identifies five formal primary sources
for each of P1, P2a, P2b, P3a and P3b, distinguishing printed formulas, model
uses and algebraic translations. The [premise audit](PREMISE_AUDIT.md) records
the experimental mismatches and the energy derivation. FBP16 supplies the
single external tutorial anchor, with the local [bridge](../tutorial/BRIDGE.md)
connecting its conventions to this result.

Versions and reading depths in this ledger bound the comparisons. D13's
recorded main-paper coverage is limited to its abstract/preview; its official
supplementary material was inspected at the depth listed above. The
[archived ledger](../archive/development/PRIOR_ART_2026-10-07.md) preserves the
retrieval history and earlier assessment sequence.
